Clipp Studio

A private publishing tool for our own YouTube channels.

Clipp Studio is an internal tool. It uploads short videos that we create to YouTube channels that we own and manage, on a schedule we set, and reads basic statistics (such as views) for those same channels.

Who can use it

Clipp Studio is not a public service and does not offer sign-up. It is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own channels.

Google sign-in

The tool uses Google OAuth to get permission to upload videos and read statistics for the connected channel. You can remove that permission at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
